# Scientific publications

### Geostatistical Characterization of Climate Extremes Dynamics: The Drought Phenomenon

Miguel Gomes, Ana Castro, Rita Durão & Amílcar Soares  
*Mathematical Geosciences*, 2026

[DOI: 10.1007/s11004-025-10248-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11004-025-10248-8)

This study characterises changes in drought severity, variability and spatial patterns in Southern Portugal over four decades. It combines complementary drought indicators, geostatistical simulation and multidimensional analysis to produce spatial drought-risk assessments and a Joint Drought Index.

### Geostatistical Predictive Model of Drought Severity: A Case Study of Southern Portugal

Miguel Gomes, Ana Castro, Rita Durão & Amílcar Soares  
*Mathematical Geosciences*, 2025

[DOI: 10.1007/s11004-025-10184-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11004-025-10184-7)

This study investigates short-term drought-severity prediction in Southern Portugal using a Random Forest model based on the Consecutive Dry Days (CDD) indicator. Recent drought conditions and historical information are used to predict drought severity, with model performance varying across drought classes and throughout the dry season.
